#### Cluster 1 Assessment
- **Empirical Corpus Maturity:** **Rich (25–45+)** when encompassing both MAS architecture benchmarks and critical SAS-vs-MAS re-evaluations; **Moderate (12–24)** specifically for strictly token/compute-matched experimental protocols (rapidly expanding across 2024–2026).
- **Baseline Quality:** **Sharp Divide.** Framework-advocacy papers frequently benchmark against greedy $N=1$ single-LLM passes and omit total token/API parity, whereas 2024–2026 diagnostic studies (`Kim et al. 2026`, `Tran & Kiela 2026`, `Smit et al. 2024`, `Wang et al. 2024`, `Self-MoA 2026`, `Kapoor et al. 2024`) impose strict token/cost/sample matching. Coding *whether baselines are compute-matched* across primary studies enables genuine meta-analytic/stratified quantitative synthesis.
- **Survey Saturation Check:** **Targeted White Space (Conditional Warning).** Pure *Multi-Agent Debate* is covered by *Motger et al.* (`arXiv:2607.26212`, July 2026, 141 studies) and *Tillmann* (`arXiv:2506.00066`, May 2025). However, **no existing survey synthesizes SAS-vs-MAS compute-matched boundaries** (capability thresholds, KV-cache simulation, homogeneous vs. heterogeneous regimes, verified vs. unverified domains, and cost-accuracy Pareto frontiers).

## 4. Strategic Topic Recommendations for Phase 2

### Primary Recommended Topic (Optimal for ACM CSUR #1 & TMLR #2)
> **Working Framing:** *"When Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Outperform Single-Agent Baselines? A Systematic Review of Compute-Matched Evidence, Coordination Failure Dynamics, and Evaluation Validity (2023–2026)"*

1. **Why It Maximizes Primary-Literature Richness:** Anchors on **Cluster 1** (`Rich` overall / `Moderate` strict compute-matched corpus, including *Nature Machine Intelligence* 2026, TMLR, ICML, ACL, and 2026 preprints) and pairs it quantitatively with **Cluster 2** (error cascades, conformity/sycophancy, failure taxonomies) and **Cluster 3** (`pass^k`, harness exploits, verification transport, cost normalization). Together, Clusters 1–3 yield **35–45+ directly comparable empirical/diagnostic studies** with concrete quantitative outcomes ($\Delta$ accuracy under equal tokens/cost, failure-rate distributions, $\kappa$, `pass^k` decay, and pp inflation).
