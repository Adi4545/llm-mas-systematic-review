### Explicit Contrast Against Classical Swarm Intelligence & Classical MAS (*La Malfa et al.*, `SR054`)
*La Malfa et al.* (`arXiv:2505.21298`, NeurIPS 2025) identify four structural divergences separating classical multi-agent systems and swarm intelligence from contemporary LLM-MAS—all directly corroborated by our 44-study extraction:
1. **Formal Agent Architectures (`BDI` vs. Stateless Script-Wrapped Prompting):** Classical MAS grounds autonomy, proactiveness, and Theory-of-Mind (`ToM`) in formal **Belief–Desire–Intention (`BDI`)** architectures. In contrast, our 24 interactive LLM-MAS studies rely primarily on stateless Python-orchestrated LLM turns conditioned on system prompts or LoRA adapters (`MADA-RL`, `SR076`). When *Szlatincsán et al.* (`SR075`, `PARTNR`) explicitly induce zero-shot Theory-of-Mind via Other Agent's Next Action prediction (`OANA`), performance **degrades in 15 of 16 model-context pairs** due to shallow planner-dependent modeling—empirically confirming *La Malfa et al.*'s warning that next-token predictors lack native `ToM`.
2. **Communication Protocols (`FIPA`/`KQML` vs. Unconstrained Natural Language):** Classical MAS standardizes formal performative protocols (`FIPA-ACL`, `KQML`) separating speech-act semantics (assert, query, refuse) from payload content. Across our extracted studies, communication remains overwhelmingly unstructured natural-language transcripts (`SR004`–`SR008`, `SR012`–`SR013`, `SR029`–`SR031`), triggering token inflation (`SR004`, `SR011`), lossy Data Processing Inequality (`DPI`) handoffs (`SR002`), "cheap-talk" debate hacking (`SR012`), and semantically benign recursive blocking attacks (`CORBA`, `SR069`) that evade content filters. Only `CMD` (`SR006` inter-group answer summaries), `MacNet` (`SR014` Markovian artifact-only edges), and `AFlow` (`SR018` typed Python operators) enforce structured message interfaces.
3. **Decentralized Swarm Intelligence (`ACO`/`PSO`/`Boids`) vs. Top-Down LLM Orchestration:** Classical swarm intelligence (`ACO` pheromone trails, `PSO` continuous particle updates, `Boids` local velocity alignment—excluded from empirical pooling in `SR057` weight-space PSO and `SR058` NetLogo ACO/Boids) achieves emergent macro-behavior through local, low-bandwidth numeric rules in asynchronous physical/grid environments. By contrast, LLM-MAS papers routinely mislabel top-down Python-loop orchestration or synchronous multi-round polling (`SR009`–`SR011`) as "emergent swarm intelligence," while *Kim et al.* (`SR001`) show unverified decentralized/independent LLM agents amplify errors up to `17.2x` without a centralized orchestrator bottleneck.
4. **Environment Coupling & Single-LLM Simulation:** Classical MAS separates objective environment physics from agent reasoning. In LLM-MAS, `SR003` (*OneFlow*) proves that homogeneous role-prompted MAS workflows collapse cleanly into multi-turn single-LLM trajectories with KV-cache reuse—demonstrating that many putative "multi-agent" pipelines lack true multi-entity environment boundaries.

### Key Structural Findings Across Topology Classes
1. **Why `Sequential Chain/SOP` and `Centralized Star` Appear Inside Multi-Topology & Diagnostic Studies:** No included study isolates a single standalone static SOP chain or centralized star without testing multiple topologies or auditing existing frameworks. Instead, `Sequential` and `Centralized` arms are systematically benchmarked inside multi-topology comparators (`SR001`, `SR002`, `SR014`, `SR019`, `SR069`) and failure audits (`SR021` `MAST` auditing `MetaGPT`/`ChatDev`/`Magentic-One`; `SR022` `Who&When` and `SR026` `TraceElephant` auditing `Magentic-One` & `Captain-Agent`).
2. **Dense All-to-All Debate (`n = 12`) Is Dominated by Conformity & Token-Starvation Failures:**
   - Almost every `Dense All-to-All Debate` paper (`11/12`, 91.7%) documents **Conformity/Sycophancy Collapse**—ranging from majority-pressure suppression in `Knight-Knave-Spy` (`SR008`), numerical/capability conformity across `>2,500` debates (`SR029`), consensus-seeking "debate hacking" (`CosMAD`/`CopMAD`, `SR012`), to contractive assimilation driving inter-agent correlation toward `1.0` and manufacturing peak overconfidence precisely on incorrect outputs (`SR079`).
   - Under finite context budgets, `SR004` demonstrates that reading peer transcripts steals internal thinking tokens (`g = W - H - c`), enabling single-agent `Self-Consistency` to match or beat `MAD` in `93%` of `980` tested configurations. Similarly, `SR005`, `SR006`, and `SR007` prove out-of-the-box homogeneous MAD fails to beat strong few-shot `CoT` or compute-matched `Self-Consistency` unless model heterogeneity (`Heter-MAD`, `SR007`), agreement modulation (`SR005`), or incentive redesign (`ColMAD`, `SR012`) is introduced.
3. **Independent/Voting Collectives (`n = 3`) Expose Quality-vs.-Diversity & Correlated-Error Limits:**
   - `MoA` (`SR011`) stacks `3–4` feed-forward layers of heterogeneous proposers into a final aggregator, incurring high latency and token bloat. `Self-MoA` (`SR010`, `N=7`: 6 proposers + 1 aggregator) refutes blind cross-model mixing by proving that sampling solely from the single best model (`WizardLM-2-8x22B` -> `Qwen1.5-110B-Chat`) outperforms heterogeneous `Mixed-MoA` by `+6.6%` on `AlpacaEval 2.0` (`65.7%` vs `59.1%`) and `+3.8%` average on `MMLU`/`CRUX`/`MATH`, because weaker heterogeneous models degrade average proposer pool quality.
   - `SR009` demonstrates that even scaling polling ensembles to `25x` inference compute fails in unverified factual domains because correlated errors across LLM families amplify shared misconceptions rather than canceling them.
4. **Adaptive Sparse / Learned DAGs (`n = 5`) Trade Inference Tokens for Upfront Amortization (`C6`):**
   - `GPTSwarm` (`SR015`: `REINFORCE` edge + node prompt optimization), `AgentPrune` (`SR016`: one-shot low-rank spatial-temporal pruning cutting tokens `28.1%–72.8%`), `G-Designer` (`SR017`: `VGAE` + task virtual node), `AFlow` (`SR018`: `MCTS` over Python code operators), and `MasRouter` (`SR019`: 3-stage mode/role/LLM policy-gradient router) all enforce **role specialization (`5/5`)** and sparsify dense communication.
   - **Architectural Trade-off:** All `5/5` adaptive DAG papers carry `C6: Unamortized Search/Training Cost Omission` flags relative to `Tier 1` compute-matched SAS, and *Xu et al.* (`SR003`, `OneFlow`) show that homogeneous workflows found by `AFlow` can be matched by single-LLM multi-turn simulation with KV-cache reuse. Furthermore, `AgentPrune` (`SR016`) and `G-Designer` (`SR017`) synthesize graphs that remain fixed during a task's execution (`no mid-trajectory re-wiring`), whereas `AFlow` (`SR018`) searches dataset-level static code graphs.
5. **Centralized vs. Hybrid Escalation (`SR001`, `SR020`) & Role Asymmetricity:**
   - Role specialization appears in `29/44` studies (`65.9%`), `13/44` lack role differentiation (`29.5%`), and `2/44` (`SR028`, `SR079`) are `"not verifiable from accessible open summary"`.
   - *Kim et al.* (`SR001`) establish an **orchestrator-capability bottleneck** (`Figure 4`): in heterogeneous centralized teams, *Claude* benefits from a weaker orchestrator coordinating stronger subagents, whereas *OpenAI* and *Gemini* degrade under weak orchestrators. Meanwhile, `CascadeDebate` (`SR020`) isolates MAS deliberation (`N=4` domain-specialized agents) strictly to marginal-confidence escalation boundaries, avoiding always-on MAS cost overhead on routine queries.

## 3. Planning, Memory, Feedback & Verification Mechanisms (`RQ2`–`RQ3`)

Extracting `F08`, `F17`, and `planning_memory_verification_notes` reveals four mechanistic levers governing whether inter-agent coordination stabilizes or collapses:

### 3.1 Memory Sharing, Context Compaction & Partial Observability
* **Markovian Artifact Isolation (`MacNet`, `SR014`) vs. Full-History Explosion:** Passing raw multi-turn peer histories triggers context overflow beyond a small handful of agents (`SR014`, `SR004`, `SR011`). `MacNet` scales past `1,000` agents in irregular/small-world DAGs by bifurcating **short-term local memory** (intra-edge actor-critic dialogue) from **Markovian long-term memory** (forwarding only final distilled artifacts along topological edges). Similarly, `CMD` (`SR006`) compresses inter-group messages to answer/count summaries while restricting full-rationale exchange to intra-group peers, whereas `OneFlow` (`SR003`) collapses homogeneous multi-agent memory into one shared KV-cache prefix.
* **Embodied Partial Observability & Knowledge Exchange (`PARTNR`, `SR075`):** In `N=2` decentralized embodied `ReAct` collaboration across `1,000` `PARTNR` tasks, *Szlatincsán et al.* (`SR075`) disentangle passive state observation (`OAS`), co-located object Knowledge Exchange (`KE`), and speculative next-action prediction (`OANA`). While `OAS` uniformly improves task success and `KE` cuts simulation steps by up to `25%`, explicit `OANA` degrades success in `15/16` model-context pairs while inflating partner text mentions (`55%–100%`)—proving that sharing grounded environmental state (`OAS`/`KE`) stabilizes coordination whereas speculative mental-state prediction destabilizes zero-shot planners.
* **Observability Loss Under Compaction & Delegation (`TraceElephant`, `SR026`; `VTFR`, `SR040`):** Lossy memory pruning or interface abstraction severely impairs downstream supervision. `TraceElephant` (`SR026`, `220` traces on `Magentic-One` and `Captain-Agent`) proves that exposing full step prompts, context inputs, and tool returns boosts failure attribution accuracy by **`+76%`** over partial output logs. Consistently, `VTFR` (`SR040`) shows that when identical trajectories are judged across abstraction boundaries, **multi-agent delegation incurs a `30.9%` Verification Transport Failure Rate**, **macro-tool composition incurs `31.0%`**, and **history compaction incurs `~20%`**, with lossless provenance restoration recovering `~85.5%` of transport failures.

### 3.2 Explicit Verifiers & Attribution-Feedback Loops
Across the 44 studies, **verification mechanisms** split into four architectural patterns:
1. **Execution & Unit-Test Verifiers (`AFlow` `SR018`, `PatchDiff` `SR036`, `WebArena Verified` `SR039`):** `AFlow` embeds `Test/Programmer` sandbox execution operators directly into discovered workflows; `PatchDiff` (`SR036`) exposes `29.6%` behavioral divergence (`6.2–6.4 pp` resolve-rate inflation) when weak benchmark tests are checked via differential testing; `WebArena Verified` (`SR039`) replaces brittle DOM/LLM-judge scoring with deterministic backend database state and network-trace verification. Conversely, `SR009` demonstrates that **without** external verification, consensus polling up to `25x` cannot separate truth from shared error.
2. **Incentive-Aligned Debate Judges (`ColMAD` `SR012`):** *Chen et al.* (`SR012`, `arXiv:2510.20963`) show that competitive (`CopMAD`) and consensus-seeking (`CosMAD`) debates suffer "debate hacking" (cheap-talk persuasion vs. premature dissent suppression). Reframing debate as a non-zero-sum game (`ColMAD`) aligns debaters to surface truthful, complementary evidence for a downstream **Judge/Verifier**, gaining up to `+10 pp` on error detection over legacy MAD.
3. **Multi-Perspective & Counterfactual Failure Localizers (`AgentLocate` `SR025`, `AgenTracer` `SR023`, `TRIAGE` `SR027`, `MAST` `SR021`):** `MAST` (`SR021`, `1,642` traces, $\kappa=0.88$) documents that `FM-3.2` (no/incomplete verification) and `FM-3.3` (monoculture same-model verification) pervade MAS frameworks (`41%–86%+` failure rates). Single-step localization in `Who&When` (`SR022`, max `14.2%` step accuracy) and long-context debugging in `TRAIL` (`SR028`, max `11%` with `Gemini-2.5-Pro`) initially failed sharply. Subsequent specialized verifiers overcome this gap via:
   - *Counterfactual Replay (`AgenTracer`, `SR023`):* oracle step substitution + multi-granular RL (`AgenTracer-8B`), providing self-correction feedback to `MetaGPT` and `MaAS`.
   - *Confidence-Aware Multi-Perspective Verification (`AgentLocate`, `SR025`):* independent evaluators critique a primary judge's attributions with iterative fine-tuning, outperforming `AgenTracer` on `Who&When` and `Aegis-Bench`.
   - *Severity-Ranked Triage & Simulated Replay (`TRIAGE`, `SR027`):* replaces single-step blame (contested by an LLM council in `59.3%` of traces) with suspicion scoring + structural-impact graph + LLM-simulated repair replay (`73.9%` top-5 on `Who&When`; `93.2%` top-10 on `TRAIL`).
   - *Corpus-Scope Boundary Note on `VerifyMAS`:* External literature includes `VerifyMAS` (*Hypothesis Verification for Failure Attribution in LLM Multi-Agent Systems*), which frames attribution as error-first hypothesis verification on `Who&When` and `Aegis-Bench`. Live verification confirmed `VerifyMAS` is **not** among the 44 studies in [included_studies.csv](file:///Users/adityasharma/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/llm-mas-systematic-review/data/included_studies.csv) and is therefore excluded from all 44-study quantitative counts above.
4. **Statistical Certification (`CMAD`, `SR079`):** To counter endogenous diversity collapse in unverified debates (`18%–47%` miscoverage under agreement-based stopping), `CMAD` (`SR079`) attaches a **split-conformal coverage certificate** guaranteeing $\ge 1-\alpha$ marginal set coverage even when peer correlation approaches `1.0`.

### 3.3 Anti-Sycophancy, Credit-Assignment & Topology Defenses
* **Dynamic Anti-Sycophancy Prompt Steering (`CONSENSAGENT`, `SR030`):** Monitors round-by-round answer consistency and agent confidence in a central memory, dynamically injecting critical prompt refinements upon stall detection to suppress "yes-men" capitulation and reach consensus in `1–2` rounds post-trigger.
* **Peer Sycophancy Priors (`Too Polite to Disagree`, `SR031`):** Supplies `6` interacting open-weight agents (`Llama-3.1/3.2`, `Qwen-2.5`) with static or dynamic peer sycophancy credibility rankings computed without ground-truth labels, dampening deference to sycophancy-prone peers and raising accuracy by **`+10.5 pp`** absolute.
* **Counterfactual Critic RL (`MADA-RL`, `SR076`):** Trains LoRA-specialized compact (`<=4B`) critics with a counterfactual critic advantage (`critic reward − generator ensemble accuracy`), forcing critics to repair generator consensus errors rather than imitate majority outputs (`p < 0.001` across 5 math benchmarks).
* **Structural Topology & Runtime Guards (`AgentPrune` `SR016`, `G-Designer` `SR017`, `GPTSwarm` `SR015`, `CORBA` `SR069`):** Sparse learned graphs (`GPTSwarm`, `AgentPrune`, `G-Designer`) prune compromised or adversarial agent edges, whereas `CORBA` (`SR069`) warns that semantically benign recursive blocking attacks propagate contagiously across `AutoGen` and `CAMEL` topologies unless **collaboration-aware runtime bounds** (dialogue entropy tracking, strict recursion/turn caps) are enforced.
