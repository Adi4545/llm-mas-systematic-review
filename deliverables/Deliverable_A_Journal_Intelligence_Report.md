### Why the Other Four Journals Were Not Shortlisted
1. **JAIR:** Blocked by its verified official pause on all new Survey Article submissions from **December 1, 2026 through January 2027** (plus *Plaat et al.* already appeared Dec 30, 2025, `10.1613/jair.1.18675`).
2. **Information Fusion:** Requires explicit multi-sensor/multi-source fusion scope, prefers pre-submission proposals, carries a `$4,740` Gold OA APC, and published *Sapkota et al. (2026)* (`10.1016/j.inffus.2025.103599`).
3. **JAAMAS:** Q2 community venue focused predominantly on original technical MAS research (surveys are rare/special-issue driven; *When Foundation Models Meet Multi-Agent Systems* CFP had early-2026 deadlines).
4. **EAAI:** Enforces strict desk-rejection rules requiring a concrete real-world engineering application alongside the AI contribution in the abstract.

## 3. Comparative Audit of 15 Published Reviews Across the Shortlisted Top 3 Journals

Full 9-dimension profiles for all 15 inspected reviews are archived in [agent2_journal_intelligence.md](file:///Users/adityasharma/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/llm-mas-systematic-review/agents/agent2_journal_intelligence.md#L194-L376). Key structural patterns observed across the top 3 venues:

- **CSUR (5 inspected: Du et al. 2026 `10.1145/3789261`; Zhang et al. 2026 `10.1145/3794858`; Geng et al. 2026 `10.1145/3817113`; He et al. 2025/2026 `10.1145/3773080`; Hu et al. 2026 `arXiv:2404.02039`):**
  - *Pattern:* Every CSUR survey relies on an **original multi-axis taxonomy** (e.g., parameter-driven vs. parameter-free optimization; framework vs. agent generalizability; organizational control $\times$ internal policy scaling; threat origin $\times$ layer $\times$ impact case studies; 6-genre game challenges) rather than chronological listing.
  - *Critical White Space:* None of the six 2025–2026 CSUR LLM-agent/MARL surveys focuses systematically on **when LLM-based multi-agent systems outperform vs. underperform single-agent baselines under cost/compute controls, inter-agent coordination overhead, and benchmark validity**.
- **AIRE (5 inspected: Ala'anzy et al. Oct 2026 `10.1007/s10462-026-11731-2`; Abou Ali et al. Nov 2025/Jan 2026 `10.1007/s10462-025-11422-4`; Chowa et al. Jan 2026 `10.1007/s10462-025-11471-9`; Hady et al. Nov 2025 `10.1007/s10462-025-11340-5`; Tang et al. 2023 `10.1007/s10462-022-10281-7`):**
  - *Pattern:* Four recent AIRE reviews explicitly employ **PRISMA**, defined **RQs**, corpus filtering counts (90, 107, 108 primary studies), and **Table 1 prior-survey comparison tables**.
  - *Crowding Warning:* *Ala'anzy et al. (2026)*, *Chowa et al. (2026)*, and *Abou Ali et al. (2025/2026)* already occupy broad "architectures + applications + governance" MAS/Agentic AI review territory in AIRE.
- **TMLR (5 Survey-Certified inspected: Huang et al. Aug 2026 `XycbogUAeJ`; Zhang et al. Jan 2026 `RY19y2RI1O`; Ke et al. Jul 2025 `SlsZZ25InC`; Sumers et al. Feb 2024 `1i6ZCvflQJ`; Mialon et al. Jul 2023 `jh7wH2AzKK`):**
  - *Pattern:* Rewards rigorous **formal/operational taxonomies** (e.g., POMDP Agentic RL, CoALA modular memory/action spaces, 3D memory substrate/mechanism/subject, inference scaling $\times$ architecture regimes) and candid critiques of benchmark overfitting, error cascades, and inference cost.

### Key Transferable Lessons (Separating Journal vs. Conference Evidence)

| Risk Dimension | Verified Public Evidence (Documented OpenReview Cases) | Transferable Safeguard for Our Systematic Review |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Claims–Evidence Mismatch & Compute Confounds** | **TMLR Paper 7332 (*AgenticNet*, Rejected):** Abstract claimed *"consistently outperforms"* despite trailing *MaAS*/*MermaidFlow* in Table 1, negative Table 2 ablations (`96.4%→83.2%`), `"strict exact-match"` re-runs dropping MBPP `100%→77.5%`, and cost-matched MATH Level 5 trailing Vanilla CoT/simpler baselines. **ICLR 2025 Sub 6097 (*SCALE*, Withdrawn):** Missing API cost comparison vs. human annotation and missing single-prompt baselines. **ICLR 2024 (*CoELA*, Score 8):** Flagged for lacking token/comm cost trade-offs. | **Core analytical lens:** Never report headline multi-agent accuracy without explicitly coding **whether single-agent baselines were compute/token-matched**, **token/API overhead**, and **exact-match/harness sensitivity**. |
| **2. Conceptual Boundary Overlap & Terminological Rigor** | **TMLR `CTr3bovS5F` (*Self-Evolving Agents*, Accepted w/ revision):** Criticized initially for blurring boundaries with Continual/Lifelong Learning and encyclopedic listing. **TMLR `SDkEq8Fzvl` (*Generalist Game Agents*):** Forced to replace overloaded `L1–L5` labels with `S1–S5` and fix Eq. 2 bounds. **TMLR Desk-Reject (*Evaluating World Models*):** Desk-rejected for high grammar errors and undefined jargon. **NeurIPS 2025 (`FfsxgSZW0c`, *Miss the Multi-agent Mark*):** Audited misappropriation of classical MAS autonomy/protocol terms for sequential prompt chains. | Define every MAS, orchestration, and swarm term upfront using non-overlapping labels; **explicitly distinguish classical swarm intelligence and classical MAS from LLM-based agent coordination and prompt chaining**. |
| **3. Proxy Metrics & Architecture–Claim Mismatch** | **TMLR Sub 7253 (*Inducing Disagreement*, Rejected):** Measured disagreement rate (`99.2%`) without downstream outcome quality, and used isolated parallel sampling to make claims about interactive "deliberation." **ICLR 2024 (*Evaluating Multi-Agent Coordination*, Withdrawn → NAACL):** Compared LLM agents only to PPO/PBT without contemporary LLM baselines, and claimed human-AI coordination from BC proxies. | Separate **process proxy metrics** (disagreement, message volume) from **task outcome quality**; audit whether baselines match the real comparator class (strong single-agent LLM vs. multi-agent LLM). |
| **4. Benchmark Ecological Validity & Error Attribution** | **ICLR 2024 (`zAdUB0aCTQ`, *AgentBench*):** Criticized for static prompt bias, simplified environments, and lack of diagnostic granularity. **NeurIPS 2025 (*MAST*), ICLR 2026 (*AgenTracer*, *Emergent Coordination*), ICML 2026 Workshop (*AgentDebug*):** Quantify high baseline MAS failure rates (`41%–86.7%` in MAST across 1,600+ traces, $\kappa=0.88$), root-cause attribution gaps, and spurious temporal coupling. | Grade primary studies on benchmark validity (prompt sensitivity, variance/`pass^k`, harness bugs) and integrate failure-attribution taxonomies rather than trusting aggregate leaderboards. |
| **5. Status Separation & Compliance Gates** | **TMLR `3hXEPbG0dh` (*Agent Harness Engineering*):** Withdrawn June 30, 2026 (NOT rejected). **TMLR `CsoSWpR5xC` (*Social Agents*):** Required explicit Broader Impact section prior to acceptance. **TMLR `5567` (*Data Science Agent*):** Upgraded around 5 progressive RQs + benchmark structural flaws. | Strictly tag every source's publication status (`Peer-reviewed journal` / `Peer-reviewed conference` / `Workshop` / `Preprint` / `Withdrawn` / `Rejected`) and include a dedicated Governance / Broader Impact / Validity section. |

## 5. Documented Access Limitations (Phase 1)
1. **Paywalled Bibliometric & Publisher Sub-pages:** Official Clarivate JCR pages (`JIF`) and dynamically rendered guideline/APC sub-pages (`dl.acm.org/journal/csur/author-guidelines`, `link.springer.com/journal/10462`, `sciencedirect.com`) could not be scraped directly; JIFs and certain word/APC figures rely on registry/secondary mirrors (`casrai.org`, `scispace.com`, `journalsearches.com`) and are labeled accordingly, while `acm.org` (2026 Open Access `$0`/`$250`/`$350` rates and GenAI Authorship Policy), `jmlr.org` (TMLR guidelines/AI footnote), `jair.org` (Dec 2026 survey pause), and `elsevier.com`/`ctfassets.net` (`$4,740` *Information Fusion* APC and GenAI policy) were verified directly.
2. **Paywalled Full-Text PDFs:** For several CSUR (`Du et al.`, `Zhang et al.`, `Geng et al.`, `He et al.`) and AIRE (`Hady et al.`, `Tang et al.`) reviews, exact corpus size/methodological numbers inside the paywalled publisher PDF were inaccessible where open-access `arXiv` preprints omitted final systematic screening counts.
3. **Confidential Traditional Journal Reviews:** Rejected manuscripts and confidential referee letters for CSUR, AIRE, JAAMAS, *Information Fusion*, and EAAI are strictly non-public; *PeerJ CS* opt-in open review texts (`10.7717/peerj-cs.3630`, `10.7717/peerj-cs.3882`) were not indexed in search snippets; and several 2025–2026 OpenReview withdrawn/pending forums restricted raw score-sheet scraping. Zero confidential reviews or missing numbers were inferred or fabricated.
