## 1. Overall Methodology Assessment & Recommendation

**Recommendation: Major Revision Required (Methodological Integrity, Search Reproducibility, and Protocol Harmonization Must Be Resolved Prior to Certification/Acceptance).**

The manuscript exhibits genuine structural strengths in within-study paired-outcome stratification (`Tier 1`–`Tier 4` baseline rigor, explicit anti-pooling rules, and three-tier publication-status sensitivity analysis for `RQ1`). Most core arithmetic across `search_log.json` (`126 - 42 = 84`), `screening_records.csv` (`84 - 30 = 54 - 10 = 44`), `Table 3` (`49` tuples), and `Table 7` (`QA` stratum means) reconciles programmatically.

However, a rigorous PRISMA 2020 and Kitchenham–Charters audit exposes **two Blocking methodological defects** and **four Significant protocol/scoring inconsistencies** that undermine the manuscript's claim to being an exhaustive, reproducible systematic literature review:
1. **Non-reproducible, post-freeze-mutated open-web searching (`126` raw hits total) that omitted 26 verified primary studies already surfaced in Phase 2** (including explicit Protocol `v1.0` anchor examples *Agent Forest* and *Gao et al.*, plus multi-agent security comparisons such as *Hagag et al.* ICML 2026 and *Lee & Tiwari*) while citing 11 unscreened external surveys as `E4` exclusions in Section 6.4.
2. **Snippet-only extraction misrepresented as Stage 2 "Full-Text Eligibility" (`I5`)**, leaving `383` missingness entries (`75` full-field technical slots across `35/44` studies) as `"not verifiable from accessible open summary"`—even on open-access `arXiv`/`ACL`/`PMLR` papers—and invalidating the headline **`0/24` comparative `pass^k`** claim (which is `0/23` verifiable with `1/24` paywalled/unverifiable on `SR001`).
3. **Undisclosed Stage 2 $\kappa$ subset shift (`n = 52` vs. `54`)**, single-screener Stage 2 bypass on `SR079`/`SR082`, inconsistent inclusion boundaries (`SR069` vs. omitted MAS security; `SR024` vs. single-agent `RQ4` audits; `SR079` two-time TMLR reject in `Table 9 Tier I`), internal `QA2`/`QA3`/`QA1` scoring contradictions, and absent sensitivity checks for preprints (`20.5%`) and workshops (`6.8%`) in `RQ3`/`RQ4`.
